## Getting Started

### System Requirements
- **Android**: Version 6.0 (Marshmallow) or higher
- **Internet Connection**: Required for all features
- **Storage**: At least 100 MB free space
- **Camera**: Required for photo attachments (optional)

### Installation
1. Open Google Play Store on your Android device
2. Search for "AU Fondue"
3. Tap "Install" and wait for download to complete
4. Open the app once installation is finished

### First Time Setup
When you first open AU Fondue, you'll need to:
1. Allow necessary permissions (camera, storage, notifications)
2. Choose your preferred language (English/Thai)
3. Sign in with your AU credentials

## Login Process

### For AU Students and Staff

#### Using Microsoft Account (Recommended)
1. Tap "Sign in with Microsoft"
2. Enter your AU email (e.g., u6xxxxxx@au.edu)
3. Enter your AU password
4. Complete multi-factor authentication if required
5. You'll be automatically logged in

#### Using Email/Password
1. Tap "Sign in with Email"
2. Enter your registered email address
3. Enter your password
4. Tap "Sign In"

### Password Reset
If you forget your password:
1. Tap "Forgot Password?" on the login screen
2. Enter your registered email
3. Check your email for reset instructions
4. Follow the link to create a new password
5. Return to the app and login with new password

### Stay Signed In
The app keeps you logged in for 30 days. You can:
- Enable biometric login (fingerprint/face) for quick access
- Sign out manually from Profile settings

## Reporting an Issue

### Step 1: Start New Report
1. Tap the "Report Issue" button (+ icon)
2. Choose issue category:
   - **Maintenance**: Broken facilities, repairs needed
   - **Cleaning**: Cleanliness issues
   - **Security**: Safety concerns
   - **IT Support**: Computer/network problems
   - **Other**: Issues not fitting above categories

### Step 2: Describe the Issue
1. **Title** (Required): Brief description (e.g., "Broken chair in Room 401")
2. **Description** (Required): Detailed explanation of the problem
3. **Location** (Required):
   - Building selection
   - Floor number
   - Room/Area specification
   - Or use "Pin on Map" for precise location

### Step 3: Set Priority
- **Low**: Non-urgent, minor inconvenience
- **Medium**: Should be fixed soon, affects daily activities
- **High**: Urgent, safety risk or major disruption

### Step 4: Add Evidence (Optional but Recommended)
- **Photos**: Up to 5 images
  - Tap camera icon to take photo
  - Or select from gallery
  - Images are compressed automatically
- **Videos**: Up to 1 video (max 30 seconds)
  - Shows the issue more clearly
  - Useful for intermittent problems

### Step 5: Review and Submit
1. Review all entered information
2. Tap "Submit Report"
3. You'll receive a confirmation with issue ID
4. Notification will be sent when status changes

### Tips for Good Reports
- Be specific about location
- Include clear photos from multiple angles
- Describe when the issue occurs (if time-specific)
- Mention if it's a safety hazard
- Check if issue already reported (to avoid duplicates)

## Tracking Your Issues

### My Issues Screen
Access via bottom navigation or home screen button

#### View Options
- **All Issues**: Complete list of your reports
- **Active**: Only pending and in-progress issues
- **Resolved**: Completed issues
- **Filter by Date**: Select date range

#### Issue Details
Tap any issue to view:
- Full description and photos
- Current status and timeline
- Assigned staff member (if any)
- Comments and updates
- Resolution details (when resolved)

#### Status Meanings
1. **Submitted**: Issue received, awaiting review
2. **Under Review**: Staff examining the issue
3. **Assigned**: Designated to specific department/staff
4. **In Progress**: Actively being worked on
5. **Resolved**: Issue has been fixed
6. **Closed**: No further action needed
7. **Cancelled**: Issue withdrawn or duplicate

### Adding Comments
You can add updates to your issues:
1. Open the issue details
2. Tap "Add Comment"
3. Type your message
4. Optionally attach a photo
5. Tap "Send"

### Issue Actions
- **Edit**: Modify details (only when status is "Submitted")
- **Cancel**: Withdraw the issue
- **Reopen**: Request reopening if not properly resolved

## Notifications

### Types of Notifications
1. **Status Updates**: When your issue status changes
2. **Comments**: When staff adds comments to your issue
3. **Announcements**: System-wide messages
4. **Reminders**: Follow-up on pending issues

### Managing Notifications
1. Go to Profile → Settings → Notifications
2. Toggle notification types on/off:
   - Push notifications
   - Email notifications
   - In-app notifications
3. Set quiet hours (no notifications during specified times)

### Notification Center
- Access via bell icon in top bar
- Shows last 30 days of notifications
- Tap to mark as read
- Swipe to dismiss

## Frequently Asked Questions

### General Questions

**Q: Is AU Fondue free to use?**
A: Yes, completely free for all AU students and staff.

**Q: Can I report issues anonymously?**
A: No, all reports are linked to your account for accountability and follow-up.

**Q: How long does issue resolution typically take?**
A: Depends on issue type and severity:
- High priority: 1-2 days
- Medium priority: 3-5 days
- Low priority: 5-10 days

**Q: Can I report issues outside campus?**
A: No, only for AU campus facilities and properties.

### Reporting Issues

**Q: Can I edit my report after submission?**
A: Yes, but only while status is "Submitted". Once assigned, contact support.

**Q: What if my issue is urgent/emergency?**
A: For emergencies, call campus security directly. Use app for non-emergency issues.

**Q: Can I report multiple issues in one report?**
A: No, create separate reports for different issues for better tracking.

**Q: Why can't I upload my photo/video?**
A: Check:
- File size (photos <5MB, videos <50MB)
- Internet connection
- Storage permissions

### Account Issues

**Q: I can't log in with my AU account?**
A: Try:
1. Check internet connection
2. Verify AU credentials work on other AU systems
3. Use email/password login as alternative
4. Contact IT support if persistent

**Q: How do I change my password?**
A: Go to Profile → Account Settings → Change Password

**Q: Can I use multiple devices?**
A: Yes, you can be logged in on up to 3 devices simultaneously.

## Troubleshooting

### Common Problems and Solutions

#### App Crashes/Freezes
1. Force close the app
2. Clear app cache (Settings → Apps → AU Fondue → Clear Cache)
3. Restart your phone
4. Update to latest version
5. Reinstall if problem persists

#### Login Issues
- **"Invalid credentials"**: Double-check email/password
- **"Account locked"**: Too many failed attempts, wait 30 minutes
- **"Network error"**: Check internet connection
- **Microsoft login fails**: Clear browser cache, try again

#### Can't Submit Report
- Ensure all required fields are filled
- Check internet connection
- Reduce image size/quality
- Try without attachments first

#### Not Receiving Notifications
1. Check notification permissions in phone settings
2. Verify notifications enabled in app settings
3. Check Do Not Disturb mode is off
4. Ensure app isn't being killed by battery optimizer

#### Photos/Videos Not Uploading
- Check storage permissions
- Ensure sufficient storage space
- Try with smaller file sizes
- Check internet speed/stability

### Error Codes
- **Error 401**: Authentication failed - Please login again
- **Error 403**: Access denied - Contact support
- **Error 404**: Content not found - Try refreshing
- **Error 500**: Server error - Try again later
- **Error 503**: Service unavailable - Maintenance in progress
